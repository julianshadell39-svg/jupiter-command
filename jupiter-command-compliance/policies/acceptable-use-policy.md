# Acceptable Use Policy (AUP)

**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective Date:** 2026-07-04  
**Owner:** Jupiter Command Security Team  
**Review Date:** 2027-07-04

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines acceptable use of Jupiter Command systems, networks, and data by employees, contractors, and third parties.

## 2. Scope

Applies to all users with access to Jupiter Command production systems, internal tools, and data assets.

## 3. Acceptable Use

- Access systems only for legitimate business purposes.
- Protect login credentials; never share passwords.
- Report suspicious activity to security@howtogiveback.org immediately.
- Use approved devices and networks when handling sensitive data.

## 4. Prohibited Use

- Unauthorized access to systems or data not required for your role.
- Installation of unlicensed or unapproved software.
- Exfiltration, sale, or unauthorized disclosure of user or platform data.
- Using platform resources for cryptocurrency mining or personal gain.
- Bypassing security controls (MFA, VPN, firewall rules).

## 5. Enforcement

Violations may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination and legal referral.

## 6. Acknowledgment

All users must sign an acknowledgment of this policy upon onboarding and annually thereafter.
