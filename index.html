    FAQ

    Can I donate monthly?

    Yes. Select “Monthly recurring” in the donation form.

    Will I receive a receipt?

    Yes. A receipt is generated immediately and sent to your email address.

    How do you report impact?

    We publish regular updates and key metrics in the Impact section.

    Privacy Policy

    We collect donor information only for donation processing, receipts, compliance, and communication. We do not sell personal data. You can request deletion by contacting support.

    Terms

    Donations are voluntary and may be tax-deductible where applicable. Recurring donations remain active until canceled by the donor. Refund requests are reviewed within 10 business days.
