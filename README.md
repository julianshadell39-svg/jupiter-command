# jupiter-command
AI Command Center for Jupiter DeFi on Solana. Built with Base44. Features wallet connect, Jupiter Terminal integration, Superagent AI for natural language DeFi commands, portfolio analytics. Bootstrapped MVP with freemium SaaS model.

## Phase 1 Focus: Financial Support and Cooperation Growth

- Map support network: family, peers, mentors, community groups, and professional contacts.
- Define two cooperation goals:
  - One accountability partner.
  - One collaboration project.
- Schedule weekly financial check-ins with a trusted partner.
- Create a shared growth plan with:
  - Income goals.
  - Responsibilities.
  - Timelines.
  - Review dates.
- Identify cooperation opportunities:
  - Joint freelancing.
  - Referrals (received and given).
  - Shared resources.
  - Group buying.

### Weekly KPIs

- Number of support meetings.
- Referrals received and given.
- Joint opportunities created.
- Extra income from collaboration.

### End-of-Month Review

- Keep high-trust, high-return partnerships.

## Monetization Strategy

### 1) Pricing Model

#### Free
- Basic portfolio tracking.
- Limited AI query quota.
- Core Jupiter workflow access with standard rate limits.

#### Pro
- Higher AI and automation usage limits.
- Advanced analytics and strategy insights.
- Priority alerting and faster support response targets.

#### Team
- Shared workspaces and collaborative operations.
- Team-level dashboards and permissioned roles.
- Cross-seat collaboration workflows with centralized billing.

### 2) Paid Feature Set

- Premium AI automations for repeatable DeFi actions.
- Real-time alerting and signal subscriptions.
- Advanced wallet analytics and performance attribution.
- Cross-protocol insight panels for consolidated decision-making.
- Priority execution and support routing for paid tiers.

### 3) Transaction-Based Revenue

- Add affiliate/referral fee flows for supported DeFi actions.
- Restrict monetized routes to integrations that explicitly allow partner economics.
- Disclose referral economics clearly in product UX and policy docs.

### 4) Subscription and Billing Architecture

- Launch in-app fiat subscriptions with Stripe as the primary billing provider.
- Support optional Solana-native payments for crypto-native users.
- Keep entitlements unified so either payment path maps to the same tier access rules.
- Maintain auditable billing events for entitlement activation, renewal, grace periods, and cancellation.

### 5) Monetization UX

- Trigger upgrade prompts when users hit quota or feature boundaries.
- Provide clear tier comparison views to reduce upgrade friction.
- Offer a time-boxed trial for paid capabilities.
- Ship a billing dashboard with plan status, invoices/receipts, payment methods, and renewal controls.

### 6) Monetization KPIs

Track and review on a recurring cadence:
- Conversion rate (free to paid).
- ARPU.
- Churn rate.
- Trial-to-paid conversion.
- Feature-level retention impact by tier.

### 7) Phased Rollout

#### Phase A: Baseline Monetization
- Ship freemium + Pro subscription.
- Validate willingness to pay and core upgrade paths.

#### Phase B: Collaboration Expansion
- Launch Team plan with workspace and collaboration features.
- Measure seat expansion and multi-user retention.

#### Phase C: Partner Revenue Expansion
- Add/refine partner referral channels after PMF signals are stable.
- Optimize referral conversion while preserving user trust and compliance.
